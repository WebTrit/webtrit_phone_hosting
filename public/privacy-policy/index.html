Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

Thank you for choosing our application! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure experience while using our VoIP softphone. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information. By using the application, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: To use the application, you may need to create an account. We collect and store information such as your username, email address, phone number, and language preferences.
  • Call Data: We process data related to your voice and video telephony calls, including call duration, participants, and timestamps, to enhance service quality.
  • Camera and Microphone: With your permission, the app accesses your camera and microphone to make audio and video calls, transmitting this media to the other party.
  • Contact Lists: With your permission, we access your contacts to enable calling them, and in some situations, this information may be uploaded to our servers.
  • Messages and Shared Content: If messaging is enabled, we transmit the messages you send and receive, which may include images and files you choose to share.
  • Push Notification Tokens: We use a device push token to deliver incoming call and message notifications.
  • Usage and Diagnostic Data: We collect diagnostic data such as crash reports, device model, operating system version, app language, and IP address to improve the stability and performance of our app and service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Improvement: We use collected data to enhance and optimize the application's performance, features, and user experience.
  • Communication: We may use your contact information to send important updates, announcements, or respond to your inquiries.
  • Security: Your information is crucial for ensuring the security and integrity of our services, detecting and preventing fraud or abuse.

3. Data Sharing

  • Third-Party Service Providers: We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers to assist us in delivering and improving our services. These providers adhere to strict data protection standards.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety, and that of our users.

4. Your Choices

  • Account Controls: You can manage and update your account information through the application settings.
  • Permissions: You can grant or revoke access to your camera, microphone, and contacts at any time through your device settings.
  • Data Deletion: Contact us to request the deletion of your account and associated data.

5. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

6. Updates to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. Check back periodically for the latest information.
